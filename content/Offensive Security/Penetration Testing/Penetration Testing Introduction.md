## 1 What is Penetration Testing?

In this section:

- A clear definition of Penetration Testing
    
- The difference between Penetration Testing and:
    
    - Vulnerability Scanning
        
    - Bug Bounty
        
- Why companies perform Penetration Testing
    

**Important note:**

- Penetration Testing is a simulation of a real-world attack, not just vulnerability discovery
    

---

## 2 Goals of Penetration Testing

Discuss:

- Identifying exploitable vulnerabilities
    
- Assessing real-world impact
    
- Testing existing security controls
    
- Improving the overall security posture

## 4 Penetration Testing Methodology (Core of the Article)

This is the most important section 👇  
Explain each phase step by step:

### 1. Planning & Scope

- Defining objectives
    
- Defining the scope
    
- Defining constraints (Rules of Engagement)
    

### 2. Reconnaissance & Information Gathering

- Collecting information
    
- Understanding the attack surface
    

### 3. Threat Modeling & Analysis

- Identifying potential weaknesses
    
- Prioritization of attack paths
    

### 4. Vulnerability Identification

- Manual testing
    
- Automated tools (with emphasis on the importance of manual testing)
    

### 5. Exploitation

- Exploiting identified vulnerabilities
    
- Proving exploitability (Proof of Concept)
    

### 6. Post-Exploitation

- Assessing real impact
    
- Data access and privilege escalation
    

### 7. Reporting

- Executive summary
    
- Technical findings
    
- Security recommendations
