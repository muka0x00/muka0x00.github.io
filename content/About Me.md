Welcome to my **Blog** — a space where I document my journey, research, and hands-on experience in offensive security.

This blog is not about theory-only content. It focuses on **how attackers think**, **how systems fail**, and **how to break things responsibly in order to secure them better**.

---

I’m an offensive security enthusiast with a strong background in:

- Web & API security
    
- Linux internals
    
- Programming (Python, PHP, JavaScript, C)
    
- Reverse engineering & low-level concepts
    
- Bug bounty & real-world exploitation mindset
    

I believe that **deep technical understanding + continuous practice** is the only real path to mastering security.

---

## What This Blog Covers

You’ll find content related to:

- **Web Exploitation**  
    SQL Injection, XSS, CSRF, IDOR, Authentication flaws, Logic bugs
    
- **API Security**  
    Broken object level authorization, mass assignment, JWT issues
    
- **Bug Bounty Write-ups**  
    Realistic attack paths, methodology, and impact-driven reports
    
- **Linux & OS Internals**  
    Permissions, processes, memory, privilege escalation basics
    
- **CTFs & Labs**  
    Walkthroughs, thought process, and mistakes to avoid
    
- **Security Tooling**  
    Custom scripts, automation, and tooling breakdowns
